# 🛒 Kovaimart Supermarket Sales Analysis & Dashboard

## 📊 Project Overview

**Kovaimart Supermarket Sales Analysis** is an end-to-end **Microsoft Excel Data Analytics and Business Intelligence project** developed to analyze supermarket sales transactions and convert raw business data into meaningful insights.

The project analyzes **800 transactions across 30 attributes**, covering sales, products, customers, stores, branches, promotions, payment methods, profitability, ratings, and returns.

The main goal of this project is to help supermarket management understand **sales performance, profitability, product performance, store performance, customer behavior, promotion effectiveness, and return patterns** through an interactive Excel dashboard.

---

## 🎯 Business Problem

A supermarket generates a large amount of transaction data every day. However, raw transaction data does not directly provide management with clear answers to important business questions.

The business needs to understand:

* Which products generate the highest revenue and profit?
* Which stores and branches perform best?
* Which promotions generate better results?
* What are the monthly sales trends?
* Which payment methods are most commonly used?
* What percentage of transactions are returned?
* Why are products being returned?
* Where are opportunities to improve profitability?

This project addresses these questions by transforming transaction-level data into **KPIs, analytical reports, visualizations, and business recommendations using Microsoft Excel**.

# 🏪 Store & Branch Analysis

Store-level analysis was performed to compare revenue and profitability across different locations.

| -------------- | ---------: | --------: |

### Key Finding

**Saravanampatti** generated the highest store revenue, while **Peelamedu** generated the highest store-level profit.

Therefore, store performance should be evaluated using both **revenue and profitability**.

# 💡 Key Business Insights

The analysis produced several important findings:

### 1. Strong Revenue Base

The business generated approximately **₹577.4K in revenue** from 800 transactions.

### 2. Profitability

Total profit is approximately **₹54K**, resulting in an overall profit margin of **9.35%**.

### 3. Product Performance

**Aavin** is the leading product based on both revenue and profit contribution.

### 4. Store Performance

**Saravanampatti** leads in store revenue, while **Peelamedu** leads in store profitability.

### 5. Promotion Performance

**Tamil New Year Sale** generates the highest revenue, whereas **Republic Day Sale** generates the highest profit.

### 6. Monthly Performance

**March** is the strongest month in terms of revenue.

### 7. Digital Payment Adoption

Google Pay and UPI represent a major share of transaction activity.

### 8. Returns

The overall return rate is relatively low at **2.88%**, but damaged packaging is the primary return issue.

# 💼 Business Recommendations

Based on the analysis, the following recommendations were identified:

### Product Strategy

Prioritize inventory availability for high-performing and high-profit products.

### Pricing & Margin Management

Review products with high revenue but comparatively lower margins to identify opportunities for improving profitability.

### Store Performance

Study the operational practices of high-profit stores and apply successful approaches to lower-performing locations.

### Promotion Strategy

Evaluate promotions based on **profit as well as revenue** before continuing or expanding campaigns.

### Digital Payments

Continue supporting reliable digital payment infrastructure because of the high adoption of digital payment methods.

### Return Reduction

Improve packaging, storage, and handling processes to reduce damaged-pack returns.

### Customer Feedback

Encourage customers to provide ratings and feedback to improve customer experience analysis.
